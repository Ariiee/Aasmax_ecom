# Database Schema Design

- `users`: customer and admin identities
- `categories`: product classification
- `products`: catalog items with inventory
- `cart_items`: active shopping carts
- `orders` and `order_items`: checkout and line items
- `payments`: mock payment records
- `addresses`: shipping addresses
- `reviews`: product feedback
- `wishlist_items`: saved products
- `banners`: homepage CMS sections
